Connect your MinimalPad, change one key, and save it in about five minutes. You do not need to install drivers, edit files, or reflash firmware.

<Note>
  No unlock step is required. When the Studio status indicator turns green, the pad is ready to edit.
</Note>

<Steps>

  <Step title="Connect your MinimalPad">
    Connect the pad directly to your computer with a USB-C **data cable**. Charge-only cables provide power but cannot connect to Studio.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Studio and select the pad">
    Open [MinimalPad Studio](https://studio.minimalmacropad.com/) in Chrome or Edge. Click **USB / Serial**, select **MinimalPad** in the browser dialog, then click **Connect**.

    <Tip>
      If MinimalPad does not appear, try another data cable or USB port. Avoid USB hubs while troubleshooting.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Inspect your current keys">
    Studio shows the live assignments for all 16 keys and the encoder. Select a layer tab, then click any key to inspect its current action.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Change one key">
    Select the key you want to change, choose an action, configure it, then click **Assign**. See [Change a key](/studio/change-a-key) for every action category.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** in the toolbar. Saving writes the keymap to the pad so it survives reboots and disconnections.

    <Warning>
      Save before disconnecting or powering off. Unsaved changes are lost after a reboot.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test your key">
    Press the physical key and confirm it performs the new action. If needed, return to Studio, adjust it, and save again.
  </Step>

</Steps>
