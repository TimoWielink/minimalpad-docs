Every MinimalPad has the same 16 programmable keys, rotary encoder, wireless features, and Studio support. Choose how much assembly you want to do and which controller you prefer.

## Kit vs. assembled

| | DIY Kit | Fully Assembled |
|---|---|---|
| What arrives | Pre-soldered PCB and loose through-hole parts | Finished, flashed, and tested pad |
| Soldering | Required | Not required |
| Switches and keycaps | Your choice | Kailh Choc Navy switches and white ABS keycaps by default |
| Controller choice | Your choice (NRF52840 clone or Nice!Nano V2) | Your choice (NRF52840 clone or Nice!Nano V2) |
| Firmware and testing | You flash and test it after assembly | Flashed and individually tested |
| Ship time | Standard | Built to order, typically around one week |
| Best for | Makers, tinkerers, budget-conscious builders | Plug-and-play users, anyone without a soldering iron |

## Which should you choose?

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Choose the DIY kit" icon="screwdriver-wrench">
    Pick the kit if building is part of the fun, you want to choose every visible component, or you want the lowest-cost option. The surface-mount components are already soldered, but you still need a soldering iron for the switches, encoder, and controller.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Choose fully assembled" icon="circle-check">
    Pick the assembled version if you want a finished device that works out of the box. Each pad is built, flashed, and tested before shipping.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  You verify a DIY build yourself. If you are new to soldering and want the lowest-risk option, choose the assembled version.
</Note>

## NRF52840 clone vs. Nice!Nano V2

Both controllers use the Nordic nRF52840 chip and provide the same Bluetooth, battery monitoring, ZMK, and Studio features.

| | NRF52840 Clone | Nice!Nano V2 |
|---|---|---|
| Positioning | Cost-effective alternative | Original community reference board |
| Community support | General ZMK community | Large, dedicated Nice!Nano community |
| Best for | First builds, cost-conscious buyers | Anyone wanting the reference-grade controller |

Choose the clone to keep costs down. Choose the Nice!Nano V2 if you prefer the original board and its larger dedicated community. Day to day, they work the same in MinimalPad.

<Note>
  The controller does not affect how MinimalPad Studio works. Both options connect and configure identically through the browser.
</Note>
