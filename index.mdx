## What is MinimalPad?

MinimalPad gives you 16 hot-swappable low-profile mechanical keys arranged in a 4×4 grid, a rotary encoder you can turn and press, and RGB underglow — all in a compact form factor that sits beside your keyboard or sits under your hand.

  <Card title="16 Programmable Keys" icon="keyboard">

## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose and receive your MinimalPad">
    Pick a [DIY kit or a fully assembled MinimalPad](/getting-started/choose-your-minimalpad) from the store. Check what you'll need [before you begin](/getting-started/before-you-begin) — a USB-C data cable and, for wireless use, a compatible battery are required and not included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and load your keymap">
    Plug in your MinimalPad, open [MinimalPad Studio](https://studio.minimalmacropad.com/configure) in Chrome or Edge, and select your device. Your current key configuration loads automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign keys and save">
    Click any key, pick an action, and hit **Save**. Changes persist directly to the device — no firmware reflash needed for everyday customisation.
  </Step>
</Steps>
