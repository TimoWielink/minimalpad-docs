### It Started With a Simple Idea

About a year ago, I decided I wanted to build my own macropad.

I’m Timo, based in Amsterdam. MinimalPad started as a side project and is now becoming real — the first 50 kits are being assembled, tested, packed, and shipped by me from Amsterdam.
