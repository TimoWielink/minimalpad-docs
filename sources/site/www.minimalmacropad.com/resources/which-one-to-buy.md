## Which MinimalPad Should You Buy?

At first glance all MinimalPads look the same. Every version uses the same PCB, the same 4x4 layout, the same rotary encoder, and runs the same ZMK firmware. Whether you choose a kit or an assembled build, you're getting the exact same MinimalPad experience once it's on your desk.

I assemble every kit and every finished unit by hand, in my own studio in Amsterdam, and ship it from there myself. There's no factory line or outsourced fulfillment behind this, it's just me at the bench, so however you decide to buy, you're getting something I've personally built or personally packed.

That's also why I try to support the kit as much as I can. I've written a full assembly guide that walks through the whole build, including a complete parts and tools list, so you know exactly what you need before you start and exactly what to do once the box arrives.

The real differences come down to how much of the build process you want to do yourself, and which controller you choose.

### Kit or Assembled?

Both versions are built around the same hardware. The choice is simply whether you want to build it yourself or have us do it for you.

### Get the Kit if...

You enjoy the process as much as the finished product.

The Barebones Kit starts at **$34.99** and includes everything required to build the pad yourself. You'll choose your controller, switches, keycaps, and case, then assemble it at home.

Building a keyboard is one of the best ways to learn basic electronics and soldering. The MinimalPad is intentionally designed to be approachable, even if it's your first soldering project. With only sixteen switches and a straightforward layout, it's significantly easier than building a full keyboard.

That said, soldering always comes with a learning curve. A cold joint, bridged connection, or incorrectly seated component can cause keys not to register properly. Because every kit is assembled by the customer, we can't guarantee it will work perfectly the moment you finish your final solder joint.

What we can guarantee is support.

If something isn't working, we'll help you troubleshoot it. Whether it's a diode installed backwards, a switch that isn't registering, or a controller that isn't flashing correctly, we'll work through the issue with you until your MinimalPad is up and running.

The kit is perfect for:

- Makers and tinkerers
- People who enjoy DIY projects
- Anyone wanting to learn keyboard building
- Users looking for the lowest-cost entry point

### Get it Assembled if...

You just want to open the box and start using it.

Every assembled MinimalPad is soldered, flashed, tested, and configured before it ships. When it arrives, it's ready to plug in, pair, and customize through ZMK.

Each unit is hand-built and individually tested on our bench. Every key is verified, the encoder is checked, firmware is flashed, and the board is inspected before packaging.

Because each assembled unit is built to order, shipping usually takes around one week.

Pricing starts at:

- **$99.99** with a Clone controller
- **$129.99** with a Nice!Nano V2

The assembled version is ideal for:

- Creators who need a tool, not a project
- Streamers and content creators
- Developers and designers
- Anyone who doesn't own a soldering iron
- Users who want a guaranteed working device on day one

### Nice!Nano V2 or Clone?

This is probably the question I get asked the most, and it's a fair one. Both controllers run the exact same ZMK firmware and are built around the same Nordic nRF52840 chip, so you get identical Bluetooth, wireless operation, battery monitoring, and every feature you'd expect from a modern wireless macropad. Functionally, you won't notice a difference once your MinimalPad is up and running, the gap is entirely in quality, reliability, and long-term support.

I include the Clone controller because it's genuinely good value, most clone boards use the same chip and perform virtually identically day to day, so if you're building your first macropad, experimenting with ZMK, or just keeping costs down, it won't let you down. I also offer the Nice!Nano V2 for anyone who wants what I'd reach for myself: it's manufactured to a higher standard, backed by a strong community, and it's the reference board that thousands of builders trust to run every day.

### A Note About Batteries

No MinimalPad ships with a battery.

Lithium batteries are heavily regulated by shipping carriers and customs agencies, especially for international orders. Shipping them globally significantly increases costs and complexity, so we've chosen not to include them.

Fortunately, sourcing a battery is straightforward.

Any standard **3.7V LiPo battery with a JST connector** will work with wireless builds. Capacity is entirely up to you.

As a rough guide:

- 110–250mAh: Smallest footprint
- 300–500mAh: Balanced size and runtime
- 600mAh+: Maximum runtime

Thanks to ZMK's extremely low power consumption, even relatively small batteries can provide weeks or months of use between charges depending on your usage patterns.

### Our Recommendation

If you're comfortable soldering and enjoy building things, get the Kit with the Clone controller. It's the most affordable way to experience MinimalPad and you'll learn something along the way.

If you want a polished product that arrives ready to use, choose the Assembled version. For most users, the extra cost is easily justified by the time saved and the confidence of receiving a fully tested device.

And if you're still unsure about the controller choice?

Start with the Clone. Upgrade to Nice!Nano V2 only if you specifically value premium hardware and want the original board that helped define the ZMK ecosystem.
