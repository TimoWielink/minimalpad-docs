# Configure Your Keys

On this page

#### **What you need**

MinimalPad runs on ZMK firmware and supports ZMK Studio live keymap editing — no reflashing every time you want to change a key. You'll need a USB-C cable and a Chromium-based browser (Chrome or Edge) that supports WebSerial.

#### **Connect your MinimalPad**

Plug the MinimalPad in over USB-C, open ZMK Studio, and select your device from the connection prompt. You'll see a live map of all 16 keys plus the rotary encoder.

#### **Remap a key**

Click any key on the on-screen layout, then choose a new keycode, shortcut, or macro from the picker. Changes apply instantly, no reflash needed, so you can test as you go.

#### **Layers**

Add a layer to fit more shortcuts onto the same 16 keys, and assign a layer-tap or momentary-layer key to switch between them on the fly.

#### **Save your layout**

ZMK Studio writes changes directly to the keyboard's onboard settings, so your keymap persists across reboots and reconnects.

If your MinimalPad doesn't show up in ZMK Studio, try a different USB-C cable (some are power-only) or a different USB port.
